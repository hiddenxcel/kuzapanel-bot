Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 June 2026

KuzaPanel ("we", "us", "our") operates a WhatsApp-based service that lets customers order social media marketing services (such as followers, likes, and views) and manage a wallet balance. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

1. Information We Collect

  • WhatsApp profile information: your phone number and WhatsApp display name.
  • Order information: the platform, service, quantity, and account/post link you provide for each order.
  • Payment information: the mobile money phone number you use to pay and the transaction reference. We do not store your mobile money PIN or full card details — payments are processed by licensed payment providers.
  • Wallet & activity: your wallet balance, deposits, order history, and referral activity.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process and deliver the orders you place.
  • To process deposits and maintain your wallet balance.
  • To send you order updates, payment confirmations, and service notifications via WhatsApp.
  • To operate our referral program.
  • To provide customer support.

3. Sharing of Information

We share information only as needed to provide the service:

  • With payment providers (e.g. Snippe, ZenoPay, HarakaPay) to process payments.
  • With service providers (SMM providers) to fulfil your orders — limited to the link and quantity required.
  • With Meta/WhatsApp as part of using the WhatsApp Business Platform.

We do not sell your personal information.

4. Data Retention

We retain your information for as long as your account is active or as needed to provide the service and comply with legal obligations.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us at the details below.
